Built for the assignment

CineGraph uses the official Neo4j Movies dataset, six parameterized Cypher templates and a local instruction model to answer movie questions with supporting evidence.

Explore casts, directors, filmographies, release details and recommendations based on shared actors.

Transparent by design

Every answer exposes its retrieved rows and query. Recommendations count shared actors; they do not predict ratings or your personal taste.

The graph is a small sample, not a complete movie catalogue. Exact names are required, and generated text should be checked against its evidence.
